Media, photography & video

Tell the story. Protect the people.

Last updated: September 2026 CTY Ministries · Phase One policy centre

A practical public-facing policy for photography, video and other media used by CTY Ministries.

People before content

CTY may use photographs and video to tell the story of ministry, celebrate community and communicate upcoming activities. The purpose of this policy is to make that documentation respectful, proportionate and safe.

"Honor one another above yourselves."

Romans 12:10

Simple rule: a good photograph is never more important than a person's dignity, privacy or safety.

Adults

Where practical, CTY should provide notice at events and activities that photography or video may take place. People who do not wish to appear in public-facing media should have a reasonable way to communicate that preference.

Children & teens

Images of children and teens require additional care. CTY should use its approved consent process, involve a parent or guardian where required, avoid unnecessary identifying details, and never publish material that could reasonably place a child at risk.

  • A parent or guardian's consent is obtained before a child's photo or video is used in public-facing media, using CTY's approved consent form.
  • Consent records are kept on file and can be reviewed or updated by the parent or guardian at any time, at no cost.
  • Captions and posts avoid pairing a child's photo with identifying details such as their school, home address or full schedule.

Event notices

Where an event will be photographed or filmed, CTY should communicate this clearly through registration information, signage, verbal notices or another appropriate method. The method should match the audience and the sensitivity of the event.

Livestreaming & recorded services

Services or events that are livestreamed or recorded may capture attendees who are seated in view of the camera, in addition to anyone specifically featured. Where an event is being livestreamed or recorded, CTY should give reasonable notice before it begins.

Anyone who would prefer not to appear in a livestream or recording may ask an usher or event host about seating outside the camera's view.

Consent choices

Where individual consent is used, CTY should explain what the media may be used for—for example, the website, social media, printed ministry materials or internal communication. Consent should not be bundled into unrelated requests when a separate choice is reasonably possible.

Withdrawal & removal

Requests to remove an image should be handled respectfully and promptly where reasonably possible, especially where the person is identifiable and the material is publicly accessible.

Removal may not be technically possible from every third-party platform or past publication, so CTY should explain practical limitations honestly.

Sensitive situations

Do not publish images or details that reveal private counselling, prayer, medical, disciplinary, safeguarding or other sensitive circumstances without a strong and appropriate basis.

Copyright & permissions

CTY should only publish photographs, video, music, graphics and other creative material that it owns, has permission to use, or is otherwise lawfully entitled to publish.

Where a contributor provides media to CTY, the intended usage and permissions should be clear before publication.

Media contact

If you have a question about a photo or video, or would like to update your family's consent preferences, reach out any time:

Media/privacy contact: privacy@ctyministries.org

Safeguarding contact: safeguarding@ctyministries.org
