This policy is designed to give visitors a clear picture of how CTY handles personal information while keeping the experience consistent with its Christian community values.

Privacy at CTY

Every family, volunteer and visitor who shares information with CTY Ministries is placing a measure of trust in us — the same trust we ask people to place in this community when they walk through our doors. This policy explains what information this website may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

"Whoever can be trusted with very little can also be trusted with much."

Luke 16:10

Christian community, without pressure. CTY may share Christian teaching and ministry information, but using this website does not require you to adopt a particular belief or participate in ministry activities.

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the website. We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed for the purpose explained to you.

01

Contact details

For example, your name, email address or phone number when you voluntarily contact CTY or submit a form.

02

Requests & messages

Information you choose to include in contact, prayer or ministry-related requests.

03

Event information

Details needed to respond to registrations or participation requests where those features are offered.

04

Technical data

Basic device, browser, usage or security information may be processed through hosting, analytics or security services.

How we use information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate about activities you requested, administer events, improve the website, protect the service, and meet applicable legal or safeguarding responsibilities.

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis or notice, and we do not sell personal information.

Children & young people

CTY serves children, teens and youth, so privacy and safeguarding deserve particular care. We avoid collecting unnecessary information from minors and expect forms, communications and media practices to follow CTY's safeguarding procedures.

"Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these."

Matthew 19:14

Welcoming children well means protecting them well. The families who trust CTY with their children's time also trust us with their information, and we treat both as a stewardship, not a formality.

Where information or consent is required for a child or young person, CTY should use an appropriate parent, guardian or legally valid consent process and should not rely on a child to provide information they are not expected to provide independently.

This website is not knowingly designed to collect personal information directly from young children without a parent or guardian's involvement. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information to us directly, please contact us using the details below so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

Photos, video & media

CTY may document ministry activities through photographs or video. Our Media & Photo Consent Policy explains the approach to consent, notices, children and withdrawal requests.

If you believe an image creates a genuine privacy or safeguarding concern, contact CTY using the official contact channel provided by the ministry.

Sharing information

CTY may use trusted service providers for hosting, forms, email, analytics, security, payments or other website functions. Where a provider processes personal information on CTY's behalf, access should be limited to what is necessary for the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect people from serious harm, or where necessary for a legitimate safeguarding or security response.

Cookies & analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences, security and—if enabled—analytics. Non-essential analytics or tracking should be configured with appropriate notice and choices where required, and you should generally be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices & rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or other handling of your personal information. Requests should be sent through CTY's official privacy/contact channel.

CTY may need enough information to verify and properly handle a request. We will not ask you to send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary credentials through an ordinary contact form.

Security, retention & breach notice

CTY should use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Information should not be kept indefinitely. Retention periods should reflect the reason information was collected, operational needs, safeguarding considerations and applicable legal requirements.

If a data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights or safety at risk, CTY will take reasonable steps to notify affected individuals and any relevant authority within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Contact & changes

Questions about this policy, or a request about your own information, are always welcome — reach out any time.

Privacy contact: privacy@ctyministries.org

Organisation: CTY Ministries

This policy may be updated when CTY's website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date above.
