Our Story

About CTY Ministries

CTY stands for Children, Teens, and Youth — a ministry built to give every generation, from the youngest child to the young adult stepping into leadership, a place to belong. Through fellowship, shared testimonies, and honest conversation, we exist to build one community that faces life's challenges together and motivates each generation to carry the faith forward.

What started as a simple idea — bringing children, teens, and youth together under one roof instead of three separate, disconnected programs — has grown into a movement built on a single conviction: no generation should walk their faith journey alone.

Why We Exist

One Family, One Journey

We believe community is formed in the ordinary moments as much as the big ones — in shared meals, late-night conversations, cake after service, and showing up for each other through both celebration and hardship. CTY exists to hold space for all of it.

What started as a simple idea — bringing children, teens, and youth together under one roof instead of three separate, disconnected programs — has grown into a movement built on a single conviction: no generation should walk their faith journey alone.

Community is not a side activity at CTY; it is the method and message. Every generation is invited to grow, be known, and move forward in faith with people who will walk the journey together.
